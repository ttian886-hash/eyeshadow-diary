# v301: complete recording flow

Review-only branch; production remains v300 until publication is authorized.

## User-visible changes

- Save from either the picker or a palette detail now opens History, selects the Looks tab, clears stale search/month filters, and reveals/expands the exact saved record, including backdated entries.
- Saving an edited record likewise reveals that record rather than leaving it outside active filters.
- The recording tray and review share one product/shade summary. Empty selection uses a direct Choose your shades prompt.
- Review spacing is tighter without shrinking touch targets. Keep choosing explicitly preserves the draft.
- History editing has a concise explanation, a live count-impact summary, and a save/cancel bar above the navigation. Unchanged, empty, or invalid-date edits cannot be submitted through the button.
- Delete confirmations name the destructive action and explain that milestones stay.
- Escape closes review options before leaving the review; closing review preserves the draft. Keyboard visibility is applied before measuring navigation height.
