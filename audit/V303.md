# v303: collection, detail and management

Review only; production remains v302 until authorized.

- Search tolerates repeated whitespace, letter case and word order across brand, product and shade names, shared by collection and recording picker.
- Empty filtered/favorite views provide direct recovery actions.
- Detail management uses readable Favorite/Edit/Archive labels and 44px targets; normal browsing no longer displays recording selection counts. Product and brand titles render as text.
- Collection rows expose their product identity so returning from detail restores focus. Archive category/status are retained in the detail origin.
- Renaming an existing product cannot create a duplicate brand/name identity. Existing usage and shade structure protection remain.

Checks: 7 collection checks, 38 app/state checks, 14 import checks, 14 mocked cloud checks passed (73 total). Scripts parse and diff checks pass. Preview uses isolated fixtures and six scenes at 320/390/768px; browser geometry results shown in preview, pending visual review at preparation time.

Not physical-device or live cloud certification. No personal cloud records were changed.
