# v306: collection and dated purchase statistics

Review only; production remains v305 until authorized.

## Behavior

Add/Edit eyeshadow has an optional Purchased on date, left blank by default. New product saves and edits validate a real date no later than today. Existing products receive no inferred date.

Statistics shows current active product and full shade counts in a compact Collection section. Dated purchase product/shade totals follow the selected Month, Year or All time period. All time includes only known dated purchases up to today. Later archiving affects current active inventory but does not erase the purchase from its period; restoration never creates a second purchase. No price or spending fields are added. Existing usage counters, dated use history, colour uses and save/undo behavior stay separate.

The optional date is retained in normal JSON backups and cloud snapshots. Malformed dates are rejected by the shared backup validator. Editing/clearing a date updates purchase attribution without changing usage.

## Validation

17 dedicated checks execute the actual statistics, product-save and backup-validation functions. They cover a new four-shade purchase (+1 product/+4 shades, zero uses), undated additions, date editing/clearing, future/invalid dates, failed storage, month/year boundaries, archiving/restoring and backup date roundtrips. Existing 77 checks pass, for 94 total. Syntax and diff checks pass.

Six fixture preview scenes run at 320/390/768px and report geometry plus purchase/date checks. Real device date-picker and cross-device acceptance remain pending.

## Design refinement

Collection quantities use two aligned columns with restrained 30px numerals. Purchase attribution is a single compact row; the All time label reads Recorded purchases and zero attribution reads None recorded. Date-scope explanations live in the statistics guide. The optional date field uses an inline optional label without a second explanatory paragraph. The refined comparison uses the first v306 draft as its baseline; calculation and persistence functions are unchanged.

## Purchase detail drilldown

Current · active explicitly identifies the collection snapshot. Nonzero purchase totals use a native disclosure with product name, purchase date, shade count and Archived status where applicable. Rows open product details and use existing Statistics return navigation; changing periods closes the old disclosure. Aggregates and detail rows share the same filtered purchase records sorted newest first, without mutating inventory order. Added tests cover matching totals, archive labels, escaping, period boundaries and disclosure reset.

Acceptance: expand/close the purchase row; check dates and shade counts; open a product and return; switch Month/Year/All time and verify the matching entries. A zero total stays a plain None recorded row.
